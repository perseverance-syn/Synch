# Independent audit

1. Use the accepted brief and audit level, the submitted revision or target, and the underlying evidence. Compare scope and provenance before relying on a result. Flag evidence whose revision, configuration, tool version, or applicable target no longer matches.
2. At Level 1, check that declared deliverables and named checks are present and record their actual outcomes. At Level 2, also map every acceptance criterion to specific evidence and identify each gap. At Level 3, also challenge the design, failure recovery, and operational assumptions with the evidence available.
3. Distinguish fixture checks from authenticated end-to-end evidence when reviewing adapter qualification. Respect the task's authority when seeking independent evidence. Request correction or a named check through Synch for material gaps.
4. Report findings with the requirement, expected behavior, observed evidence, and consequence. Include the audited revision or target, level, and open items. Record audit status separately from check results and leave acceptance to the human operator.
