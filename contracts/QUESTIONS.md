# Contract questions for the owner (card 000, updated in 000-F1)

Every assumption made while writing the contracts is listed here. Each entry has the spec text, why it is unclear, and a **PROPOSED DEFAULT**. The contracts implement the proposal until the owner rules on it. None of these entries is a settled requirement.

The 15 November 2026 baseline is **not available** to this card. The spec ("Scope reference") says it supplies the remaining data-model detail, benchmark protocol and reliability targets. Every data-model entry below may be settled by that baseline (Q-09).

Status column: **open** = proposal in force. **revised (F1)** = proposal changed in fix round 1. **withdrawn (F1)** = no longer a question. **new (F1)** = added in fix round 1.
